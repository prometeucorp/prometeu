# ADR 0027 — companion devices in the relay

Date: 2026-09-08
Status: Accepted

Uses an additive field in [relay v4](../contracts/relay-v4.md). Complements
[ADR 0021](0021-cloud-organizations.md) and
[ADR 0022](0022-end-to-end-encryption.md).

## Context

A phone needs to enter the relay's room with its own key. The relay stores one
key per member and the owner does not encrypt boxes for themselves; a browser
with the same membership would replace the Mac's key and would not receive
content. At the same time, the Mac's identity must stay stable: changing the
member's ID would invalidate TOFU links and make old comments unreadable.

## Options considered

1. **Several keys per member.** It changes the envelope (`boxes` per key) and
   TOFU stops detecting a replacement without cross-signing between devices.
2. **Every member becomes a device (v5).** It migrates the Mac's identity,
   requires a stable device ID across re-logins and rewrites the persistence.
3. **A companion device as an additional member.** The Mac keeps the membership
   as its identity; browsers join as their own members linked to the person by a
   `person` field in the roster.

## Decision

Option 3. The Cloud has `Companion`: a person's browser or additional Mac, with a
`relay_id` generated by the device, a label and
`last_seen_at`. `POST /orgs/:slug/companion-ticket`, authenticated by cookie and
CSRF, registers or finds the companion and issues a ticket tied to it.
`POST /api/relay/authorize` returns `member` equal to the companion's ID and the
roster with `{ id, name, person }` for each device. The first Mac identifies the
membership; additional Macs use desktop-authenticated companion tickets as
defined in [ADR 0036](0036-second-mac-as-companion.md). Browsers use a cookie
session and CSRF for their companion tickets.

`Member.person` is optional and additive in v4. Old parsers ignore the field and
see the companion as one more member. The relay validates that `person` points
to a primary member of the same roster, without chains, and persists the field.

In the client, audiences and mentions still name people. The encrypted channel
expands each person into their devices when choosing the boxes' recipients, in
the audience published in the relay and in the mentions. The owner accepts a
device through the person it belongs to when validating `watch` and `write`,
and runs that device's input only after approving its key
([ADR 0090](0090-approved-remote-input.md)). The
interface chooses the audience and mentions among people (`people()`). The
members list also shows people since [ADR 0041](0041-members-list-shows-people.md);
keys stay pinned per device, because the TOFU link is per key.

The Cloud's roster keeps the relay's 64 members: people always get in;
companions take the remaining slots in order of recent use. Each person keeps at
most five companions; the oldest is dropped when another is registered. Removing
the device in the account's settings takes it out of the roster; new content
stops being encrypted for it within 60 seconds, and comments it wrote stop being
readable by the others, as happens with removed members.

## Consequences

- No identity migration on the desktop. Existing links, comments and audiences
  stay valid.
- Legacy teams based on a shared secret have no `person`; each browser there
  would be its own membership, as today.
- Each recipient costs one box; an organization of 64 people has no room for
  companions. Raising that limit requires wrapping the content with a per-message
  key instead of one copy per box, which is another envelope and security review.
- A companion's TOFU is per device. Clearing the browser's storage creates a new
  device; the old one stays in the roster until it is dropped by age or removed
  manually.
- The mobile shell uses this identity model through
  [ADR 0028](0028-mobile-web-app.md).

## Evidence

- `relay/src/cloud.test.ts`: a roster with `person`, pending links and chains
  rejected, persistence in the reducer.
- `src/team-channel.test.ts`: the companion receives the person's share and
  mention, talks to the owner; a third party outside the audience still gets no
  content.
- `prometeu-cloud/test/integration/organizations_test.rb`: companion ticket,
  roster, isolation between people, removal and the roster's limit.
